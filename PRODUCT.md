# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Incoming Stanford GSB MBA students, from the moment they're admitted through their first term on campus. They're using this in short, scattered bursts — checking a task off between other admit-season logistics, looking up one acronym someone just used in a WhatsApp group, comparing two housing options at 11pm. They're often anxious about a big transition (new country, new city, new social codes) and currently piecing together the same information from a static PDF, a class WhatsApp/Slack, and word of mouth. Some are international students layering visa/SSN/banking logistics on top of everything else.

## Product Purpose

Replace the scattered PDF-plus-group-chat status quo with one calm, fast reference hub: the full survival guide, an international-student guide, a pre-arrival checklist that persists in the browser, a jargon dictionary, a housing comparison, and class FAQs — all searchable from one place (Cmd+K). Success looks like a student finding the specific answer they need in seconds, not scrolling a 40-page PDF, and finishing the pre-arrival checklist with less anxiety than they started with.

## Brand Personality

Warm and reassuring — the voice of a second-year who's already been through the chaos and is looking out for you. Calm confidence, not corporate polish and not manic energy. Plain, human language over institutional formality. Comfortable with GSB's own insider humor (TALK, FOAM, MARRS, Touchy Feely) without being try-hard about it.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboard** — cold, template-y admin-panel chrome with no personality. This is a guide, not a B2B tool.
- **Stuffy university PDF/handbook** — dense bureaucratic walls of text with no visual relief or hierarchy.
- **Over-designed marketing site** — big gradient hero theater and scroll-driven flourish that gets in the way of finding the actual answer fast. The existing gradient-heavy hero landing is a candidate for toning down under this principle.

## Design Principles

- **Speed to answer over spectacle.** Every surface optimizes for the fastest path to the specific chapter, term, task, or comparison — decoration never competes with findability.
- **Calm authority, not corporate coldness.** Warm materials and a human tone throughout; avoid anything that reads as cold SaaS/admin-panel chrome.
- **Respect the reader's time and anxiety.** Clear hierarchy, scannable structure, short units of text — never a wall of undifferentiated content.
- **One voice, many surfaces.** The guide reader, dictionary, checklist, housing explorer, and FAQ should feel like one coherent tool, not six bolted-together mini-apps.
- **Show the insider knowledge, don't bury it.** GSB-specific jargon and traditions are the product's real value — surface them so they're delightful to discover, not intimidating to decode.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: solid color contrast in both light and dark themes, full keyboard navigation (including the Cmd+K search and checklist interactions), and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`. No known specialized user needs beyond that baseline.
